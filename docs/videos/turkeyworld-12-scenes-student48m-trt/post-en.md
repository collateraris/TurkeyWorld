I trained my own V-JEPA-style world model for TurkeyWorld on a single NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090. I distilled the 300.1M-parameter teacher into a 47.9M-parameter student. The original game is on the left. On the right, the student predicts what happens next from the controls, map, and its own recent predictions. A 4.19M-parameter neural renderer draws each state at 32×32. A 2.98M-parameter upscaler restores wall detail at 128×128 and composites the original enemy, weapon, and hand sprites. With TensorRT, the full neural pipeline averaged 53.4 FPS across combat, pursuit, and navigation.
